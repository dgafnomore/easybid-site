Frequently Asked Questions

What is EasyBid?

EasyBid is an offline iPhone estimator for contractor labour and materials.

Where do estimates, companies, rates, and materials stay?

They stay on the phone.

Does EasyBid use cloud sync?

No. EasyBid does not use cloud sync.

Does EasyBid use an account?

No. EasyBid does not use an account.

Which devices can run EasyBid?

iPhone only. EasyBid requires iOS 17.
